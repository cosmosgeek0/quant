WHAT WAS BEING TESTED
- Your volume thesis: dead tokens that suddenly get bought. The question the commander set: if the machine enters on its own alarms and exits by simple rules, does it make money after real fees, out of sample?

WHAT WAS BUILT TO ANSWER IT (all on the Mac, no live money)
- Pulled Binance's public history: six months of 1-minute candles for all 659 tokens, plus the raw trade tape (29 GB) for the minutes before every real pump.
- Census: 3,429 real pumps in six months (+25% in a day on 3x volume). 846 HELD, 2,583 COLLAPSED (78% give back half or more within a day). 64% launched from the bottom of their yearly range — your dead-low class is where pumps start.
- Signals before the pump: in the two days before real pumps, 4 of 5 "absorption" signals fired (buying without price moving, smaller prints, price pinned). Median lead time ~47 hours. BUT the same signals fire before the ones that collapse too — nothing measured could tell HELD from COLLAPSED in advance.
- The raw tape did show your accumulation signature (smaller prints, pinned price, bursty arrivals; AUC 0.78 base-vs-control) — but coarse cutoffs gave only ~7% precision.
- Then the money test: enter on the machine's actual alarms, exit with the ladders. Result: LOSES 6 to 9 percentage-points per symbol-day at every setting; 62% of entries stop out. From KNOWN pump bases the same ladders win 99% — so all the money is in picking the base, and nothing measured picks it.
